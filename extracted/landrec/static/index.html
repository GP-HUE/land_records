📋 AI Approval Center

AI recommendations are proposals only — nothing consequential executes until you approve it. Every proposal is re-authorized server-side, checked against its before-state, executed only once, and written to the audit trail.

The Administrator remains the final authority. If the target record changed after the AI drafted the proposal, approval fails safely ("stale proposal") and the record is left untouched.
